## Visual world — “Atlantic Ledger”

**Scene:** Night air over Agadir’s coast; a private club ledger opened on bone paper; champagne-gold rule lines; ink so deep it reads as navy-black.

**Color strategy:** Restrained neutrals + one precious metal accent (committed accent, not rainbow).

| Token | Hex | Role |
|-------|-----|------|
| `ink` | `#0B1C2C` | Primary text, wordmark, dark grounds |
| `bone` | `#F7F4EF` | Page / logo ground |
| `champagne` | `#C4A574` | Hairlines, monogram, accents only |
| `mist` | `#E8E2D9` | Soft dividers, cards |
| `white` | `#FFFFFF` | Pure contrast panels |
| `success` | `#2F5D50` | Rare positive (occupancy) — never neon green |

**Do not use:** purple gradients, terracotta-on-cream cliché stacks, glassmorphism glow, emoji in brand marks.

### Typography

| Role | Face | Notes |
|------|------|-------|
| Display / logo | **Cormorant Garamond** | Quiet editorial serif; readable hairlines on photo heroes; modest tracking on GREATIMMOB |
| UI / body | **Source Sans 3** | Clear grotesque for French owners on mobile; weight 400–700 |
| Micro / labels | Source Sans 3 Medium, tracked small caps | `GESTION LOCATIVE`, section eyebrows |

**Retired (do not use):** Bodoni Moda, Manrope — too thin / low contrast on coastal photography and dark overlays.

**Rules**

- Prefer **wordmark** on light (`bone`) headers.
- Prefer **monogram** on dark (`ink`) squares / social.
- Clear space = height of the letter **G** around the mark.
- Never stretch; never add drop shadows or bevels.
- Subtitle under lockup: `GESTION LOCATIVE` only — not “Agence immobilière”.
